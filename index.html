Validated-by-Design for AI

Proving Control of AI in GxP, Continuously

Why This Matters

Artificial intelligence is changing how organisations analyse information, support decisions and automate business processes.

In regulated environments, these opportunities must be balanced with requirements for control, accountability, traceability and evidence.

Validated-by-Design for AI explores how organisations can establish risk-based governance and continuous assurance throughout the AI lifecycle.

Classify before you build. Lock what must be locked. Prove the model and the human continuously.
